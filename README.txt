DAEMONS: RIFTBOUND V6 — CASTLES OF THE FRACTURED WORLD

THE EASIEST WAY TO PLAY:
Upload index.html alone to your GitHub Pages repository. It includes the complete game, the 110 Daemon animations and UI and plays in a browser on phone or computer. The optional application-install features require the complete package files.

TO INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE:
Upload ALL of the following to the ROOT of your GitHub Pages repo: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, and the icons/ directory. Keep the names and directory structure. Open your GitHub Pages HTTPS URL on your phone, tap Install Game at the title screen or use your browser's Add to Home Screen option. Remove/re-upload older service worker files if the update appears stale. Saved games live in that browser profile's local storage; clearing site data erases saves, so avoid doing so.

STORY:
Choose Flambeast, Aquabit or Leafpup. Your new partner saves you from a wild Shadowling in the forest. Explore the starter town from the user's hand-drawn map, and nine more towns of the same world size. Each town has homes, a training hall, hospital, store, forest encounters and a royal castle. Recover its lost heirloom and complete a capture or route duel to challenge the king/queen or their top knight. Collect all ten legendary gear pieces to reach the Worldrender, a dangerous Daemon behind world-spanning earthquakes.

CONTROLS:
Computer: arrows or WASD to walk the world, E interact, Shift sprint, M menu. In battle: arrow left/right moves, arrow up jumps, arrow down guards, A/S/D/F attacks, Shift dash, C binding necklace capture (only when a wild enemy is below 38% HP), T swap team member, G tonic, R selected gear technique, Q secret Echo (when unlocked).
Phone: D-pad on left; actions on right. Battle capture button lights up when a weakened wild Daemon can be caught. Switch your active gear technique in the Gear menu.

TRAINING:
At the Training Hall try timed punching, sprint and endurance challenges. Each stat can receive 8 small permanent improvements per Daemon. Press the action button when the marker is in the green window.

NOTES:
The whole game runs inside index.html and saves automatically in your browser. The app-install package is for phone home-screen installation. Real-device GitHub Pages installation remains for user verification. This game is a playable extended adventure, not individually hand-animated production artwork; several motions are reconstructed from the original padded Daemon sprites.
